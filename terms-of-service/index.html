1. Acceptance

2. About IdleCraft

IdleCraft is a Discord bot that runs an AFK Minecraft bot for you. You register a Minecraft server from a control panel in Discord, and your bot joins it, stays connected, moves around, mines and fights mobs, eats, chats, and reconnects when it is disconnected.

IdleCraft also gives Discord server staff tools to run their community: moderation commands, AutoMod, custom commands and auto-responses, welcome and goodbye messages, logging, scheduled announcements, and general utilities.

IdleCraft is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord, Mojang, or Microsoft. It is not open source, and its code is not publicly available.

By registering a server you agree to this. The Privacy Policy explains what is shown and where. Do not register a server whose address you do not want shared.
