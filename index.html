IdleCraft Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 14, 2026

IdleCraft is a Discord bot that runs AFK Minecraft bots from a control panel in Discord. This policy explains what data IdleCraft handles, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. By using IdleCraft you agree to this policy and to the Terms of Service.

1. The short version

  • IdleCraft stores only what it needs to run your bot: your Discord user ID, the Discord server you registered in, the Minecraft server address and port you entered, your bot's username, and a generated in-game password.
  • IdleCraft does not read, store or sell the content of your Discord messages. It works through slash commands, buttons and forms.
  • When your bot joins a Minecraft server, the full server address is posted in the official IdleCraft Discord server so other members can join (see section 5).
  • You can delete your registration at any time with the Delete button on the panel.

2. Data we store

When you register a bot:

  • Your Discord user ID
  • The ID of the Discord server you registered through
  • The Minecraft server address and port you entered
  • Your bot's username (the one you chose, or a default)
  • A random password for the bot's account on that Minecraft server. You never choose or see it, and it is not your Discord or Minecraft account password.
  • Whether your bot is set to run, and when it was last used. This lets bots resume after a restart and lets us remove unused registrations.

When a server administrator sets up a panel with /panel setup:

  • The server ID, panel channel ID and panel message ID
  • The optional access role, staff role and log channel IDs
  • The ID of the administrator who ran setup, and timestamps

When we block a server for abuse:

  • The server ID, the reason, who added the block, and timestamps

3. Data we handle but do not store

  • Discord interactions. Button presses, slash commands and form input are processed to carry out your request.
  • In-game chat. Your bot is a Minecraft client. It reads chat from the Minecraft server it is on, in real time, to spot login prompts and queue messages and to choose a short canned reply. IdleCraft does not save that chat or forward it to Discord.
  • Join events in the official server. In the official IdleCraft server only, when someone joins, IdleCraft briefly mentions them in the welcome channels and deletes the message. It uses only the new member's user ID and stores nothing.
  • Operational logs. Our hosting provider keeps logs with bot names, Discord IDs, status events (connected, kicked, reconnecting) and errors. We use them for troubleshooting and abuse prevention.

4. How we use data

  • Run your bot and keep it connected, including restarting it after a redeploy
  • Show panels and check permissions
  • Send you status updates
  • Keep one bot per user and one registration per Minecraft server address
  • Remove unused registrations
  • Troubleshoot problems and prevent abuse
  • Answer support requests

5. Where your data appears

  • Log channel. If your Discord server has one, IdleCraft posts status messages there: your mention, your bot's name, a partly hidden server address, and events such as connected, kicked or reconnecting, including the kick reason sent by the Minecraft server. Anyone who can read that channel can see them.
  • Direct messages. IdleCraft messages you when your bot joins, stops or gives up. If your DMs are closed, it mentions you in the log channel instead.
  • Public join announcement. When your bot joins a Minecraft server, IdleCraft posts the full server address and port, your Discord mention and your bot's name in an announcement channel in the official IdleCraft Discord server. This happens even if you registered through a different Discord server. Repeat joins to the same address are announced at most once every 10 minutes. If you do not want a server address shared, do not register it.
  • Bot head image. Status messages show a Minecraft head image loaded from mc-heads.net, using your bot's username in the image address.

6. Third parties

  • Discord carries all bot messages and interactions.
  • MongoDB Atlas hosts our database, which is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • HidenCloud hosts the bot.
  • The Minecraft server you register receives your bot's username, its generated password (through the in-game /register and /login commands), and everything the bot does and says in game. That server's owner can see and log it, and their own policies apply.
  • mc-heads.net serves the bot head image in status messages.

We do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics trackers.

7. Who can access your data

  • Your registration belongs to the Discord server you registered in. Other servers' panels and administrators cannot view or control it.
  • Administrators of the server you registered in can stop your bot with /stop.
  • The IdleCraft owner and staff of the official server can see who has an active bot and can stop or remove registrations for support and abuse prevention.
  • We share data with the providers above to run IdleCraft, and with authorities when the law requires it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Delete button. Pressing Delete on the panel removes your registration and stops your bot straight away.
  • Automatic cleanup. A registration that has not been started for 7 days, and whose bot is not connected, is deleted automatically.
  • Staff removal. Staff of the official server can remove a registration with /unregister.
  • Server settings. Panel, role and log channel settings are kept until an administrator changes them or asks us to delete them.
  • Block records. We keep them as a moderation history.
  • Logs. Hosting logs are kept for as long as our hosting provider retains them.

To ask for access to or deletion of your data, contact us in the support server: https://discord.gg/MfwFebzdpc. We may need to verify who you are, and we may keep records we are legally required to keep or need to prevent abuse.

9. Security

IdleCraft keeps its tokens and database credentials in environment variables, generates passwords with a secure random generator, checks administrator permissions every time a command is used, and keeps each Discord server's data separate from the others. No online service is completely secure, and we cannot control how a Minecraft server handles the data your bot sends it.

10. Children

IdleCraft is not for anyone under 13, or under the minimum age Discord allows in your country. If you think a child has used it, tell us in the support server and we will remove their data.

11. International processing

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as IdleCraft changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and we will announce major changes in the support server.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: the support server at https://discord.gg/MfwFebzdpc.
